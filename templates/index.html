Chapter help

Chapters use the open transcript's language and are saved with it. Select a translation first for chapters in that language.

Automatic tries Groq, then Gemini and OpenAI. An explicit choice is tried first, with availability fallbacks. Configure API keys on the server, not in the browser.

Chapters follow subjects and learning objectives; sections follow meaningful subtopics. Examples, demonstrations and continuations stay together. Detail changes topic granularity, not a chapter count or duration. No time-based splits, merges or section caps. Exports reuse saved sections without extra AI calls.

Grouping, intervals, timestamps and formats are in Export chapters.

Slide detection options

Detail levels

More keeps each build. Balanced merges small builds. Fewer merges builds and quick flips. Full slide changes are always kept.

Lower values detect more hard cuts. Higher values detect fewer cuts.
Scales with the video, with a 2 s floor for short clips.
Slide limits

A fixed limit applies at every detail level. Short clips may include up to 10 slides. At the limit, the most distinct changes are kept.
